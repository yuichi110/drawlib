# Foundations Guide

The **Foundations** section covers core drawing concepts and fundamental building blocks in Drawlib.

---

## Foundation Modules

- [Canvas & Dimensions](./canvas.md): Coordinate space, canvas configuration, resolution, and exports.
- [Coordinates & Alignment](./coordinate_align.md): Cartesian coordinate space, geometric center anchors, and alignment transforms.
- [Shapes Overview](./shape.md): All 21 shape primitives, dimensions, and path vector shapes.
- [Circle-like Shapes](./shape_circle.md): Circles, donuts, wedges, fans, regular polygons, and stars.
- [Rectangle-like Shapes](./shape_rectangle.md): Rectangles, ellipses, arcs, parallelograms, rhombuses, and trapezoids.
- [Block Arrow Shapes](./shape_arrow.md): Straight, L-shaped, U-turn, arc, and polyline block arrows.
- [Shape Styling](./shape_style.md): Fills, borders, line styles, opacity, and embedded text styling.
- [Lines Guide](./line.md): Straight lines, curved splines, Bezier curves, and arc lines.
- [Line Styles](./line_style.md): Width, color, dash styles, and arrowhead markers.
- [Text Guide](./text.md): Horizontal and vertical text, font registration, alignment, and rotation.
- [Icons Guide](./icon.md): Phosphor icons, Google Cloud Platform (GCP) official architecture icons, and badges.
- [Images Guide](./image.md): Image embedding, PIL Image integration, Dimage caching, and filters.
- [Dimage Image Processing](./dimage.md): Image caching, transformation effects (resize, crop, blur, mosaic, colorize), and format conversions.
- [Coding Best Practices](./programming.md): Layout patterns, center alignment strategies, and clean illustration code.
